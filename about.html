About YAC Sinjhoro

Who We Are

A youth-led committee turning unity, discipline and faith into real community action.

A Community of Young People, United in Purpose

Youth Action Committee Sinjhoro brings together young people who choose to serve their town, hold themselves to a standard of discipline, and let their faith guide how they treat others. We are not built around individual recognition — we are built around a shared commitment to show up for the issues that matter to our community, again and again.

Together We Act. Together We Rise.Youth Action Committee — Sinjhoro

Rizwan Chaudhery

Founder — YAC Sinjhoro

A MESSAGE FROM OUR FOUNDER

Small Steps Today,
A Better Tomorrow.

“

I believe that real change begins when people come together with a shared purpose and a willingness to serve. Our youth have the potential to make a positive difference, and they deserve opportunities to learn, lead, and contribute to society.

Through Youth Action Committee Sinjhoro, our aim is to encourage unity, responsible citizenship, cooperation, and meaningful community service. Every positive action, no matter how small, can help us build a stronger and more hopeful future for Sinjhoro.

Let us work together, support one another, and turn good intentions into actions that benefit our community.

Rizwan Chaudhery

Founder, YAC Sinjhoro
